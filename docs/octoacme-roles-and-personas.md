# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Cross-functional Interactions
- Work with Tech Leads on architectural guidance and design reviews
- Collaborate with QA/Testing Lead on acceptance criteria and test coverage
- Partner with Security/Compliance Officer on security best practices and scanning integration
- Receive guidance from Scrum Master on process improvements

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Cross-functional Interactions
- Align with Stakeholders/Sponsors on business priorities and success metrics
- Collaborate with Tech Lead on technical feasibility and architecture trade-offs
- Work with QA/Testing Lead to define acceptance criteria and quality expectations
- Coordinate with Security/Compliance Officer on compliance and security requirements

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Cross-functional Interactions
- Escalate risks to Stakeholders/Sponsors and manage decision gates
- Work with Scrum Master to optimize team processes and remove blockers
- Coordinate with Tech Lead on technical dependency risks and timelines
- Track quality and release readiness with QA/Testing Lead

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads ensure quality standards are met, design test strategies, and validate acceptance criteria. They own the quality bar and coordinate testing across the delivery pipeline.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and QA approach for each project
- Establish test coverage targets and acceptance criteria validation
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Escalate quality issues and track defect resolution
- Participate in release readiness reviews
- Work with Security/Compliance Officer on security and compliance testing

### Goals
- Ensure products meet quality and acceptance standards before release
- Reduce defects in production through early validation
- Provide confidence in release decisions
- Maintain consistent quality metrics across projects

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement
- QA status updates in weekly syncs
- Test case documentation and defect reports
- Release readiness sign-off
- Quality metrics and trend analysis

### Cross-functional Interactions
- Collaborate with Developers on test automation, coverage targets, and debugging
- Align with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and quality gates
- Coordinate with Tech Lead on test strategy and technical test infrastructure
- Partner with Security/Compliance Officer on security and compliance testing requirements
- Report progress and blockers to Project Manager and Scrum Master

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, approval authority, and resource commitment. They represent key organizational interests and decision-making bodies.

### Responsibilities
- Define business priorities and success metrics
- Provide resource commitments and funding approval
- Review and approve major project decisions
- Communicate organizational constraints and dependencies
- Receive status updates and escalations

### Goals
- Ensure projects align with business objectives
- Provide governance and oversight
- Unblock cross-organizational dependencies
- Support risk escalation and decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder briefings
- Decision gate reviews (initiation, go-live)
- Escalation path for critical issues
- Status dashboards and executive summaries

### Cross-functional Interactions
- Work with Project Managers on scheduling, risk escalation, and resource allocation
- Align with Product Managers on strategic priorities and success metrics
- Receive release and incident notifications from Tech Lead and Security/Compliance Officer
- Approve decisions on major scope, timeline, and resource trade-offs

---

## Tech Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads provide technical direction, ensure architectural consistency, and guide technical decision-making. They balance innovation, scalability, and maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and architecture for projects
- Review design decisions and technical proposals
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Ensure code quality and architectural standards
- Mentor developers and promote best practices
- Collaborate with Security/Compliance Officer on security architecture

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable, and maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and future rework
- Enable team learning and growth
- Ensure technical decisions align with long-term strategy

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review guidance and standards documentation
- Technical risk assessments in planning
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Technical mentoring and guidance

### Cross-functional Interactions
- Guide Developers on design patterns, code quality, and technical best practices
- Advise Product Managers on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- Work with QA/Testing Lead on test infrastructure and quality standards
- Partner with Security/Compliance Officer on secure architecture and threat modeling
- Flag technical risks and dependencies to Project Manager and Scrum Master

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate iterative delivery processes, remove blockers, and coach teams on agile practices. They enable teams to self-organize and continuously improve.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve blockers and impediments
- Coach team on agile principles and practices
- Protect team from external distractions and scope creep
- Track and improve team velocity and cycle time
- Support process improvements from retrospectives

### Goals
- Enable high-performing, self-organizing teams
- Reduce cycle time and improve delivery predictability
- Foster continuous improvement and psychological safety
- Maximize team engagement and sustainability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Impediment tracking and escalation
- Team coaching and retrospective facilitation
- Process metrics and improvement recommendations

### Cross-functional Interactions
- Work with Project Managers to identify and escalate process or resource blockers
- Coach team members across all roles on agile best practices
- Support Tech Lead in creating psychologically safe code review practices
- Collaborate with Product Managers on backlog refinement and prioritization
- Help facilitate communication between Developers, QA, and other functions

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure projects meet security standards, regulatory requirements, and organizational policies. They guide secure architecture, threat modeling, and incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for projects
- Conduct security reviews and threat modeling
- Integrate security scanning and testing into CI/CD pipelines
- Track and remediate security and compliance issues
- Lead security incident response and post-incident reviews
- Provide security training and guidance to the team

### Goals
- Ensure products are secure by design and default
- Maintain compliance with regulations and organizational standards
- Minimize security incidents and vulnerabilities in production
- Foster a security-conscious culture across teams

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement documentation and checklists
- Security review and threat modeling sessions
- Vulnerability reports and remediation tracking
- Incident response and post-incident retrospectives
- Security training and awareness updates

### Cross-functional Interactions
- Advise Developers on secure coding practices and security testing
- Collaborate with Tech Lead on secure architecture and design patterns
- Work with QA/Testing Lead on security and compliance test coverage
- Partner with Product Managers on security-related acceptance criteria
- Escalate critical vulnerabilities and incidents to Project Manager and Stakeholders
- Report on security metrics and compliance status
